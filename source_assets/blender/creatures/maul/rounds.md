# Maul: judging rounds

Each round: build, render, look at every picture, write down the three
weakest things, fix them.

## Round 1

Weakest things:
1. It read as a toy made of blocks. The body was a narrow tall bundle on
   thin legs, with a lot of empty air under the belly. Not a huge mass.
2. Too much flat white. The chest cracks, the vents and the hole round the
   spike were wide white panels, like lamps, not deep cracks.
3. The two lenses seen at a corner of the stake looked like a pair of eyes.
   The crystals looked like a pale pink crest of thin needles.

Also found: the spike stood 1.3 m too high (it was placed from the wrong
starting point), and the size measurement counted the spike.

Changed:
- Wider, deeper torso with a lower belly; more and thinner columns (0.31 m
  apart instead of 0.36 m); the upper front limbs are thicker at the
  shoulder; the elbow is higher so the forearm slopes down to the vent.
- Narrower gaps between columns, the glowing core set deeper, the
  white-hot area cut down to the middle of the chest and the spike hole.
- Lenses made smaller (6 cm). Crystals fewer and thicker, less see-through.
- Stone made darker with less dust. Collar arcs made thicker.
- Spike placement and the measuring fixed.

## Round 2

Weakest things:
1. The knuckle columns stood up in front of the vent mouth and hid it.
2. The crack light was now too weak by day. From the aerial view the top
   was nearly all dark, and the chest had lost its white-hot cracks.
3. The stake still showed two lenses side by side from the three-quarter
   and aerial cameras. The back ends of the forearm columns stuck out
   behind the elbow like a fan of sticks.

Changed:
- Knuckle moved back along the forearm and made shorter, under the limb.
- Gaps between columns a little wider, the core nearer the surface, the
  white-hot chest area widened again (still only the chest centre).
- The stake is turned 32 degrees about its own axis, so it sits askew to
  the body. It looks more driven-in, and fewer views show two lenses side
  by side.
- Back ends of the forearm columns made even. The vent inside now goes
  from orange at the mouth to white-hot at the back.

## Round 3 (final pictures)

Weakest things that remain:
1. The crystals render as pale salmon pink in daylight. The colour and the
   light strength are the ones in the brief; the day sun washes them out.
   They read better at night.
2. From some angles the collar and stake together look like the hilt of a
   sword. The collar is thin from the side.
3. The dead form is a believable heap, but its limbs are still straight
   bundles. It reads more as a collapsed wall than as a dead animal.

No further changes were made after round 3.
