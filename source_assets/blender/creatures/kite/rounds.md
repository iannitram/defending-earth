# The Kite: judging rounds

Each round: build, draw every picture, look at each one, name the three
weakest things, change them.

## Round 1

Weakest things:

1. The skin read as an opaque orange card. The vein bump looked like cracked
   mud.
2. The beads were big white balls and the threads were thick as rope.
3. The four lenses were blown out to plain white.

Also seen: holes shaped like blocky plus signs, tears with stair steps.

What changed:

- Skin made more see-through. Bump made much weaker.
- Beads made smaller and dimmer. Threads made thinner.
- Each lens got an amber core disc set back behind dark glass.
- Skin grid raised from 48 to 64 steps with a jittered layout. The tear
  pattern was smoothed. Holes were stretched into long rips.
- Dead form: calmer broken spars, tighter thread tangle.

## Round 2

Weakest things:

1. The skin was still a flat graphic orange. The veins looked like evenly
   sized cells and the sheet was board-flat.
2. The lens cores read pink-white, not amber.
3. The outline was a stiff straight-sided diamond, like a road sign.

Also seen: the bone of the dead form was too white.

What changed:

- The four edges now bow inward between the spar tips, as a stretched skin
  does.
- More billow between the spars (3 cm at the front, about 7 cm at the back).
- Vein cells made long and uneven, with a stronger wander.
- Lighter see-through tint. Darker glass over the lens cores.
- Darker bone on the dead form.
- Top picture: the soldier now stands half under one wing, so the skin can
  be judged as see-through.

## Round 3

Weakest things:

1. The lens cores still read salmon pink.
2. Part of the dead skin sank 13 mm into the ground.
3. The Kite flew too high. The thread tips were at 6.0 m, not the body. The
   body was at 7.3 m, and the dashed line in the size picture stopped at the
   thread tips.

What changed:

- Lens glass changed to a dark smoked tint. The cores now read amber. The
  core colour and strength are unchanged (`#ffa01e`, emission 6).
- Nothing in the dead form goes below 4 mm above the ground.
- Flight height is now measured on the body only (skin, spars, cage,
  lenses). Lowest body point is 6.0 m. Thread tips hang to about 4.5 m.
- The dashed line now runs from the cage down to the ground.
- Top picture: soldier moved further under the wing.

## What is still weakest after round 3

1. In the four day pictures from soldier height (`01`, `02`, `03`, `05`) the
   Kite is small and nearly edge-on. That is true to its size and height,
   but those pictures say little. `09_top`, `10_close` and `07_detail` carry
   the design.
2. The skin reads see-through from above (the soldier and ground show
   through it) but at night it goes dark and flat. Only the beads read.
3. The lens cores show as lit half-discs inside deep barrels. From far away
   the cage reads as a black box, not as four amber eyes.
