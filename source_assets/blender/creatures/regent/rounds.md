# The Regent: judging rounds

Each round: build, draw the pictures, open every picture, name the three
weakest things, fix them.

## Round 1

Looked at: front, side, three-quarter, aerial, night, detail, dead.

Three weakest things:

1. It read as a stick toy. Limbs were straight poles with ball joints.
2. The ring read badly. The slot most in view was the empty one, and the
   arcs were too thin to see from the standing camera.
3. The cloth hung like stiff planks and the gold marks were too faint.

Also seen: one upper arm looked like it was waving, the lobes were a tiny
cluster, the bone cradle under the dome was a flat dish, the body was
2.05 m deep (too deep for the sheet), and the dead body floated 14 cm
above the ground.

Fixes:

- Limbs now thin in the middle and swell at each joint. Bone plates flare
  at their ends.
- Arcs made thicker. The empty slot moved low on the back.
- Cloth given folds, a sway and frayed ends. Gold marks made stronger.
- Upper arms re-posed: forward, half raised, inside the footprint.
  Depth is now 1.80 m.
- Lobes heaped into a mound that fills the dome floor. Cradle made deeper.
- The dead body is now placed by its true lowest point. It lies on the
  ground.

## Round 2

Looked at: front, three-quarter, night, detail, dead.

Three weakest things:

1. Bone cracks were too dark and too even. The plates looked like tiles.
2. The lobes were chalk white, like a bowl of balls. No sense of flesh.
3. The ring had spokes, so it read as a wheel. From the front it looked
   lopsided.

Also seen: the detail camera cut off the five lenses, the four legs were
too alike, and the dead strut hung in the air.

Fixes:

- Bone cracks made finer, paler and uneven.
- Lobes given a pink flesh tint with veins. The glow stays `#ffd9c8`.
- Spokes removed. The arcs now sit on one thin hoop held by a short fork
  behind the neck. Slots re-ordered so the front view is balanced.
- Detail camera pulled back and lowered so the lenses are in the picture.
- Knee heights, plate lengths and bow of each leg now differ a little.
- The dead strut lowered so it rests on the ground.

## Round 3

Looked at: front, three-quarter, night, detail, dead.

Three weakest things:

1. The head plate was a flat plank, not a blade.
2. The dead body was a tidy bundle of straight sticks, all pointing one
   way.
3. The hips were weak. Four long legs met a body with almost no mass
   there.

Fixes:

- Head plate now broad at the root, narrow lower down, with a chisel point
  past the lenses, a deeper curve and a taller ridge.
- In the dead form the two front shins now fold back across the rear
  legs. The body is 2.90 by 1.98 m.
- Hip plates made larger and the thigh roots thicker.

## Final check

All eight pictures drawn again at full quality and opened. Numbers are in
`size.json`.

Still weakest after three rounds:

1. The legs are very thin (6 cm, as the job asks). From far away the lower
   body can vanish against a busy background.
2. The dead form is still easy to read as a pile of poles. The dark dome
   is the only part that says "Regent" at a glance.
3. The gold diagrams on the cloth are only clear close up.
