# Cinder: judging rounds

Each round: render, look at every picture, name the three weakest things,
fix them, render again.

## Round 1 (all nine pictures)

Weakest:
1. **The glow was pink-white, not orange.** The whole core was at full
   strength, and the white-hot areas were far too large. The small one
   looked like a pale lamp.
2. **The crust looked like airbrushed plastic.** Every plate had a wide,
   smooth orange glow round its edge. The plates were smooth pillows. The
   large one looked like a toy shell.
3. **The six-sided columns were black, flat and exact.** That is the
   enemy's look, which is the one thing a Cinder must never have. Also
   the small and middle sizes looked like balls, not worn cubes, and the
   sparks were big pale lozenges.

Changed:
- Core: a darker cooling skin over most of it, full fire only in veins,
  white-hot only in the deepest dents.
- Crust: the edge glow moved down to the bottom of the plate walls and
  made weaker. Plate tops chipped with noise. Stronger bump.
- Columns: uneven widths, heights and angles, worn sloping tops.
- Shape: less ball, more cube. Small size: fewer, more varied flakes.
- Sparks: less than half the size.
- Dead form: pieces tipped further.

## Round 2 (pictures 03, 07, 08, 09)

Weakest:
1. **The glow was still salmon pink** over most of the open core, and the
   white-hot spots were flat hard-edged blotches.
2. **The dead form had thin fins.** Plates that hung over a crack showed
   their open underside. The crystal buds stuck out like nails.
3. **The dead form read as an open clam of flat slabs**, not as a cracked
   cube.

Changed:
- Core skin made darker and wider, white-hot edges softened.
- Dead form: plates on a crack removed; crystals cut to short stubs.

## Round 3 (all nine pictures)

Weakest:
1. **The dead form was worse.** With the pieces tipped far over, the flat
   broken faces pointed at the camera and almost no crust showed. It read
   as loose slabs.
2. **The glow was better but still pale** in the close picture.
3. **The large size is mostly dark from above.** It reads as a dark lump
   with orange cracks, where the two smaller ones read as embers.

Changed:
- Dead form: pieces now only part a little (5 to 15 degrees, 4 to 10 cm),
  so it still reads as one squarish lump with clear cracks.
- Core skin darker again. The glow is now orange in every picture.
- The large size was left as it is: the brief asks for heavier crust.

## Round 4 (all nine pictures, final)

Looked at every picture again. What is still weak is listed in the
report: thin shards inside the dead form's cracks, the large size's dark
read from above, and the row not sitting neatly in the squares.
