# Tender: judging rounds

Each round: build, render every picture, look at each one, name the three
weakest things, fix them, render again.

## Round 1

Three weakest things:

1. It looked like a toy robot. Limbs were smooth tubes with ball joints.
2. The head read as a snow globe with a one-eyed bird face under it.
3. The cloth strips hung like stiff planks. The dead form was a broken
   pose with a solid black dome. The model was also over the limit
   (43,860 triangles, then 60,716 after a first try at detail).

What changed:

- Limbs rebuilt as bundles of thin sinew strands over a dark frame.
- Torso rebuilt as a ribbed frame with flesh between the ribs.
- Dome made deeper, with a flesh mound and roots under the lobe. The lobe
  made lumpy instead of a smooth ball.
- Lens made smaller and moved low on the head plate, so it does not read
  as an eye.
- Plates given points and chipped edges. Cloth made narrower and ragged.
- Detail removed where it did not show (limbs, feet, seam, dome, ribs,
  cloth) to get under 40,000 triangles.

## Round 2

Three weakest things:

1. A flat tray showed under the lobe. The lobe still looked like an egg,
   and the lens still read a little as an eye.
2. The ribs were as even as a hose. The knuckles were round and glossy.
   From the air the figure was hard to read.
3. The dead form could not be read, and the body floated above the
   ground because the ring was holding it up.

What changed:

- Tray replaced by a rounded mound. Lobe given deeper folds.
- Ribs made uneven in length and spacing. Knuckles made smaller and
  duller.
- Dead form: the body is dropped to the ground on its own. The ring is
  then bent over at its mount until it rests on the ground (about 32
  degrees). The dead dome is clear and a little grey, not black, so the
  grey lobe can be seen.

## Round 3

Three weakest things:

1. Flesh was too red and bright. It should be the dark plum from the
   brief.
2. Plate cracks were as regular as a turtle shell.
3. The stance was wrong twice. First too wide and bow-legged. After that
   fix the feet were only 0.22 m apart, not the 0.3 m in the brief. The
   dead picture was also taken from too far away.

What changed:

- Flesh colour now runs from a darker shade up to `#3a2430` and no
  lighter.
- Cracks use larger, warped cells and thinner lines.
- Legs are knock-kneed: knees close, shins angled out. Feet measured at
  0.30 m apart (x = +0.149 and -0.149).
- Dead camera moved closer. Head curled less in the dead pose.
- All ten pictures rendered again and looked at after these fixes.

## Still weakest after round 3

1. The head plate still reads a little like a bird's beak from the side.
2. The dead form is a tangle from game distance. It reads as "small and
   curled" only up close.
3. The cloth strips and their gold marks are hard to see from the front.
