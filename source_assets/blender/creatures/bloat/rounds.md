# Bloat: judging rounds

Each round: build, render every picture, look at each one, name the three
weakest things, fix them, render again.

## Round 1

Three weakest things:

1. It was a smooth orange egg. The skin was too thick to see through, so
   the ribs showed as tan stripes, not dark. The top was too pointed. The
   band round the middle looked like the join of a toy capsule.
2. The veins were too few and too thin to see from any distance. The
   patch was a clean white sticker with a shine on it. The feelers were
   stiff pale rods, all the same.
3. Night: the electrical life was bright white squiggles, like chalk
   marks. Dead form: the tears had stepped, saw-tooth edges, the stain
   could not be seen, the feelers were hidden under the skin, and the
   skin looked like a paper bag.

What changed:

- Skin made much more see-through when you face it, and less at the
  edge. Colour pulled back from orange toward amber-grey. Cloudy thicker
  areas added. The shine reduced to a slight sheen.
- Top made rounder. Ribs made a little wider. The skin now bulges more
  between the ribs.
- Veins made wider, more trunks, more branches.
- Patch made darker, rougher and thicker at the rim, no shine.
- Feelers made thinner and darker, with more sway.
- Arcs made hair-thin and fewer.
- Dead skin: tears are now part of the outline, and the rim of the big
  split is pulled onto a smooth line. A wide rip opens over the bottom
  hub so the feelers show. Stain made darker.

## Round 2

Three weakest things:

1. Still read as a decorated egg. The grooves at the ribs did not show,
   so the skin did not look tied down to a frame.
2. The patch read as a white fluffy ball with long spiky stitches. With
   one round disc and "lashes" it was close to looking like an eye.
3. Dead form looked like a flower with even petals. The skin was thick
   and shiny like leather. The stain was a hard dark ring. The feelers
   on the living form still hung like parallel wires.

What changed:

- Rib grooves made sharp and deeper, ring groove sharper, shoulders
  fuller. The skin now sits in sixteen tight pillows between ribs and
  ring. More mesh round the sac so the grooves hold.
- Stitches made shorter, thinner and flatter. Patch darker.
- Feelers trail a little to the rear, each by a different amount.
- Dead form: lower arches, uneven rips, skin hugs the ground sooner, no
  shine, more see-through, finer creases, thinner veins. Stain made
  blotchy with a soft edge.

## Round 3

Three weakest things:

1. The patch was still a round disc with stitches standing out all
   round. Too close to an eye.
2. Night: the arcs were scattered marks all over the skin. They did not
   read as a flicker running along two ribs.
3. Dead skin had long straight cut edges.

What changed:

- Patch is now a rough oval set askew (30 cm long, about 20 cm wide).
  Stitches are slanted like a whip stitch and lie along the seam.
- Arcs are longer broken lines that hug two ribs closely.
- Small ragged noise added to the edge of the dead skin.

## What is still weak after round 3

1. The dead skin still looks stiffer than a wet membrane should. It is
   one draped sheet, not two layers folded on each other.
2. The murk inside is very faint by day. It shows mostly as cloudy
   patches in the skin and as a darker lower half.
3. The stretch marks are easy to miss. At game size they will not be
   seen at all.
