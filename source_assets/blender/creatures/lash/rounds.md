# Lash: judging rounds

Each round: build, render all eight pictures, open and look at each one,
name the three weakest things, fix them, render again.

## Round 1

Weakest things:

1. The legs and arms looked like a white ball-and-stick toy. Every leg was
   the same, with thick red stripes like candy.
2. The weed was an even fringe, like a comb.
3. The detail picture was aimed wrong and missed the mask. The night arcs
   were too thick and too bright.

Changes:

- Limbs now swell at the joints, with a small dark joint ball. Each leg
  differs a little in thickness and knee height.
- Red lines made much thinner. Hide made darker and mottled.
- Weed hangs in clumps of mixed length.
- Arcs made thinner and fewer. Detail camera aimed at the mask and halo.
- Trunk section changed from round to a flattened shield shape.

## Round 2

Weakest things:

1. The hide was still chalk white in sunlight.
2. The dead form was wrong: legs spread out instead of curled in, and the
   trunk lay too straight.
3. The mask was a smooth blank shape, and the spines had red tips like
   matchsticks.

Changes:

- Hide made greyer and darker, with dark wet flanks.
- Dead trunk now curls 8 degrees at each segment. Dead legs fold in over
  the belly. Hands, strut pad and bells no longer sink below the ground.
- Mask and shoulder plate given cracks and stains.
- Red spine tips removed. Fingers made thinner.

## Round 3

Weakest things:

1. The weed was too bright a green, too wide, and too regular on the lit
   side.
2. From the front, the legs on each side bunched into one flat fan.
3. The dark joint balls were too large and read as dots along the legs.

Changes:

- Weed made darker, about half as wide, with more strands in each clump.
- Each leg now has its own lean and splay, so the legs fan out unevenly.
- Joint balls made smaller. Knee heights varied more.

## Round 4 (small)

One thing fixed after the full-quality render:

- The red sacs looked like glossy sweets. They are now duller, with far
  less shine. The colour is unchanged (`#8c1f1a`).

## Still weakest after the last render

1. Dead form: the legs fold in, but read as bent sticks more than a tight
   curl. The mask and halo are small and hard to find in the picture.
2. The sacs still read brighter than the rest in direct sun.
3. Front view is crowded: the bipod stands straight in front of the body
   and the halo hides the top of the mask. The hide is still pale in sun.
